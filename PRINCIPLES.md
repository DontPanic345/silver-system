# Principles

Short, general aphorisms distilled from doing the work — not aspiration (see
`NORTH_STARS.md`) and not history (see `JOURNAL.md`). A principle earns its
place here by being genuinely reusable across experiments, not just true of
the one it came from.

Add to this list when a new one gets earned the same way. Don't add a
restatement of one already here in different words — that's a sign it
belongs as a note under the existing entry instead.

---

### Only when the big universe is sufficiently full will the small world be believable.

Believability is not a rendering problem — it comes from the rules underneath
being genuinely full, not from what the camera happens to show. A world whose
rules stop just past the visible edge reads as a trick, however good the
visible part looks.

*Origin: the user's own dictation, `dictation-dumps/agentic-development.md`
("Only when the big universe is sufficiently full, Will the small world to
be believable."). Copied into `night-shift`'s `CLAUDE.md` (commit `d83f3b0`)
and `night-shift/skills/cycle-contract/SKILL.md` §9 — both since shelved
along with the rest of `night-shift`, but the principle itself outlived the
process that stated it.*

### The only way to go fast is to go well.

Cutting corners to move faster produces work that has to be redone, which is
slower, not faster, once the redo is counted. `night-shift`'s own
closeout — a process that spent ~12 lines of log per line of shipped
logic — is a case of violating this in the other direction: "doing it well"
was over-interpreted as "documenting every step," which is its own way of not
going fast.

*Origin: `dictation-dumps/agentic-development.md` ("The only way to go fast,
is to go well."), copied into `night-shift`'s `CLAUDE.md` and
`cycle-contract` the same way as above.*

### Trust and verify.

Not "trust, then verify" as a sequence, and not "verify instead of trusting"
— both halves hold at once. This repo's headless-verification convention
(numbers/JSON a test can assert on, not a screenshot eyeballed once) is this
principle made concrete.

*Origin: `dictation-dumps/agentic-development.md` ("Trust and verify."),
stated twice in the same dump (also: "Agents decisions should be trusted and
verifed"). Copied into `night-shift`'s `CLAUDE.md` (commit `d83f3b0`) but
not into `cycle-contract`'s own Principles list (§9), which omits this one.*
